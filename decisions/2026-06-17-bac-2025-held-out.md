# BAC 2025 held out: not indexed (leakage prevention)

Date: 2026-06-17

## Decision

The **2025 BAC série C** mathematics paper (`bac-2025-maths-tc`) is the
**held-out evaluation year** and is **removed from the retrieval index**. Its
entry was deleted from the corpus build's `documents` list
(`experiments/2026-06-10-official-corpus-build/config.yaml`). The OCR output
stays staged under `data/ocr/bac-2025-maths-tc/` for query provenance, but no
bac-2025 chunk enters `data/corpus/chunks.jsonl`.

Sessions **2022 and 2024 remain indexed** as in-distribution worked material.

## Rationale

The 9 `bac2025-*` queries in `data/eval/queries.jsonl` are drawn from this
paper. If the paper is indexed, those queries can retrieve their own source
items — and, once corrigés are added, their own solutions — which would inflate
retrieval metrics (recall/MRR/nDCG) and end-to-end generation scores with
leaked gold. "Held-out year" only means something if that year was never in the
index. Until this change, it was: 6 bac-2025 chunks (`bac-2025-maths-tc-0000`,
`-0002`..`-0006`) were present in the v2/v2.1 corpus.

## Why delete the entry rather than flag it

Considered keeping the document with an `index: false` flag for self-documenting
provenance. Rejected: a flag is mutable state that can be flipped back to `false`
and silently reintroduce the leak, whereas a deleted entry cannot leak unless
someone deliberately re-adds it. The provenance the flag would have provided is
preserved instead in (a) a comment at the deletion site in `config.yaml`
forbidding re-adding it, (b) DATA_CARD §5.9, and (c) this record. Net: same
leakage protection, lower risk.

## What changed

- `experiments/2026-06-10-official-corpus-build/config.yaml`: `bac-2025-maths-tc`
  entry removed from `documents`; replaced with a "held-out, do not index"
  comment; description updated to "2 indexed BAC papers (2022, 2024)".
- `data/corpus/chunks.jsonl`: rebuilt, **728 → 722 chunks**. The 6 removed lines
  are exactly the bac-2025 chunks; all 722 retained chunks are **byte-identical**
  to before (verified via `git diff`: 6 deletions, 0 insertions). Content-type
  deltas: `theory` 190 → 185, `exercise` 35 → 34 (bac-2025 was 5 theory + 1
  exercise); all other types unchanged.
- `experiments/2026-06-10-official-corpus-build/results.json`: regenerated
  (12 documents, 722 chunks).
- `data/corpus/DATA_CARD.md`: composition table, content-type table, per-document
  table, provenance table, new §5.9, and changelog v2.2 updated.

## What this does not change

The CIAM textbook chunks and the 2022/2024 BAC chunks are untouched. The eval
query set (`data/eval/queries.jsonl`) is unaffected — the BAC-2025 queries store
their own text and reference the held-out year by metadata (`held_out_year`,
`source_detail`), not by retrieving bac-2025 chunks. `gold_chunks` stay empty
(to be filled by teacher annotation). The embedding cache keys on a model+corpus
hash and recomputes automatically on the corpus change.

## Reversibility

Re-add the `bac-2025-maths-tc` block to `config.yaml` and rebuild. There is no
reason to do this for the held-out year; if a future evaluation needs a
*different* held-out year, swap which BAC session is omitted here and rebuild.

## Follow-up (separate task, not done here)

The 2026-06-17 handoff paired this de-index with "OCR sessions 2020 + 2021 and
add them to the corpus." That is an **additive** task requiring the raw PDFs and
a Mathpix/marker OCR run (cost + lead time) and is independent of fixing the
leak. It is deferred, not part of this decision.
