## M10. Generation-side and student evaluation (specified, not executed)

Two further evaluations were designed but not conducted by the time of the report, because both depend on a
(query, response) gold set. 

(i) A response judge (the original
H3, reframed to H3b) scores a full tutor answer on four model-independent criteria
(factual accuracy, curriculum alignment, pedagogical quality, language register),
variant-blind and based on a correct human solution; its harness is built and
unit-tested but unrunnable without response-gold and a finalised rubric. It is the instrument that would test the H2 generation-side claim: whether the theory-plus-example mix yields better answers, not just better retrieval (which §M5.4 stresses cannot be concluded from retrieval metrics). Used to score frontier and small-model answers, each with and without retrieval, it would also test H4.

 
(ii) A small
field study with 10–20 Terminale C students (fixed problem set scored for
correctness; pre/post confidence; blind pairwise A/B; short interview) would provide
student-side H2 evidence; nothing is built and it needs a cohort and ethics turnaround
unavailable before the deadline. Both are carried as future work and the full protocols are in
Annex M (M.D).
